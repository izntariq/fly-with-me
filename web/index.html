This is the full adult fruit-fly brain wiring diagram (FlyWire v783) running as a leaky integrate-and-fire network (Shiu et al., Nature 2024). Music is piped into the fly's real auditory neurons in Johnston's organ. The fly's descending & motor neurons play a synth and work the mixer.

Left ear hears deck A, right ear hears deck B (headphone cue). The fly steers the crossfader toward the ear it prefers. Drop your own tracks anywhere on the page, or use the built-in demo tracks.
